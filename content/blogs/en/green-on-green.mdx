**Green on green** is variegation within *Monstera deliciosa* — lighter green tissue over a deeper green blade, without white or gold sectors.

## Light

Keep it near a bright window and out of harsh midday sun. Soft light dulls the contrast between the two greens; strong indirect light keeps the pattern readable.

## Water

Let the top of the mix dry, then water thoroughly. The lighter tissue is a little thinner, so wet feet punish this plant faster than a fully green sibling.

## Climbing

When internodes start to stretch, give it a moss pole or board. Mature leaves on a support grow larger than the same plant in a hanging pot.
